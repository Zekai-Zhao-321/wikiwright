# Sowing carrots

## Steps

1. Water the drill the evening before sowing.
2. Sow thinly and cover with a board until the seedlings show.

## History

- 2026-05-03 — Sown under a board for the first time.
- 2026-06-11 — Thinned to a finger's width.
