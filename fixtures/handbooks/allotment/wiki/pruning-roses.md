# Pruning roses

In an arid garden roses are pruned lightly, in autumn, once the heat has
passed and the plant can recover before the next dry season.

## Steps

1. Water deeply the day before you prune.
2. Remove dead and damaged stems at their base.
3. Shorten the longest stems by no more than a quarter, leaving the leaves that shade the canes.
4. Spread a mulch around the base to hold moisture in the soil.

## Notes

Hard pruning in summer exposes the canes to sunscald.
