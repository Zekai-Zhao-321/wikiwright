# Watering beans

Climbing beans drop their flowers when the soil dries out. Steady watering at
the root keeps them setting pods through the hottest weeks.

## Steps

1. Water early in the morning, before the soil warms.
2. Soak the ground at the base of each plant rather than wetting the leaves.
3. Water again when the top few centimetres of soil are dry.

## Notes

A mulch of straw keeps the roots cool and halves the watering.
