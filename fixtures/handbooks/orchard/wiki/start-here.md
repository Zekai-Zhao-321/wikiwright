# Start here

This handbook covers fruit trees and roses in a temperate garden, with cold
winters and mild, damp summers.

## Steps

1. Find the task in front of you and read its page from start to finish.
2. Check that the page's climate matches your garden before you follow it.
3. Note anything that did not work as written, and send it to the handbook's maintainers.
