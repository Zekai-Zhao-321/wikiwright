# Thinning apples

An apple tree sets more fruit than it can ripen. Thinning the clusters gives
larger apples and a steadier crop from year to year.

## Steps

1. Wait until the natural drop of small fruit in early summer has finished.
2. Remove the central fruitlet of each cluster, which is often misshapen.
3. Leave one or two fruits per cluster, about a hand's width apart.

## Notes

Thin a young tree more heavily: it cannot carry a full crop yet.
