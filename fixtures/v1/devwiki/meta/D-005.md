# D-005 — A relation is a labelled edge, and coverage is a graph query

## Context

A bundle carried "this page relates to that page" three times — body
wikilinks, a `sources:` frontmatter field, and `- <label> [[X]]` relation
items — and the graph saw a label in none of them, so a bundle's coverage was
a grep over the pages that bypassed the parser the arms use
(`packages/core/src/generate/index.ts`, 2026-09-05).

## Decision

A grammar declares an `edges` hook: an item's page references, each under the
label the edge carries (`packages/core/src/modules/index.ts:320-330`). The
relations module registers it in five lines
(`packages/core/src/stdlib/relations.ts:211-222`). The generator walks every
non-prose section through the same `grammarBindings` and `parseSections` the
arms use, asks the grammar that owns each item's kind, resolves the target
through the name index, drops what does not resolve and what points at the
page itself, and emits `{from, to, kind, label}`
(`packages/core/src/generate/index.ts:130-144`). The graph is a set — two
identical lines are one edge — and an edge carries no line
(`:36-41`, `:78-80`). `graph edges` queries the same construction over the
working tree, never the artifact on disk, so a drifted `generated/` cannot lie
to it (`packages/cli/src/verbs/graph.ts:1-5`; 2026-09-05).

## Consequences

- This bundle's "done" condition is a query: `wikiwright graph edges --label
  mapped_in --inbound subsystem --missing` must be empty.
- The manifest's `by_kind` and `by_label` counts and the per-page `inbound`
  and `outbound` adjacency, nested by label under a labelled kind, are derived
  from the same edges (`packages/core/src/generate/index.ts:224-258`).
- The four kernel edge kinds are reserved: a kit's item kind spelled
  `wikilink` is refused at load (`packages/core/src/modules/index.ts:640-641`).
