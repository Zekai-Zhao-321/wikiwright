# D-004 — No lane and no check in the code kit

## Context

A module may register grammars, vocabularies, checks, lanes, fragments, types,
templates, skill fragments and contributed entries
(`packages/core/src/modules/index.ts:491-521`). A check sees the page and the
registry and never the filesystem (`:449-479`), and a queue lane is a human
queue that only a finding routing to it consumes
(`packages/core/src/passes/index.ts:9-19`). The mechanism that makes a code
wiki honest — a `pin` measured by `freshness` against the origin the page
names, with `covers` naming the paths whose diff makes the page stale — is the
kernel's (`packages/kit-code/index.js:11-15`).

## Decision

`@wikiwright/kit-code` registers declarations only: nine abstract types under
`code/`, the `code/anchored` fragment, four relation labels contributed into
the standard library's `relations` vocabulary with ranges, two `require` rows,
a template per type and four skill fragments. No check, because "the covered
path exists" is not a predicate a kit may write — it is what `freshness`
measures. No lane, because no arm of the kit's would route to it. No
`supersedes` label, because the kernel's `supersedes` field and `retire
--superseded-by` already carry succession (`packages/kit-code/index.js:20-27`,
`:48-55`; 2026-09-07).

## Consequences

- The purity scan has nothing to refuse and the determinism fixture proves the
  declarations alone: the shipped fixture plants three findings — a subsystem
  no source map locates, a relation pointed outside its range, a `covers` path
  that leaves the repository — and expects exactly those
  (`packages/kit-code/fixture.json`).
- `modules list --root devwiki` reports `checks: []` and `lanes: []` for the
  kit, and that is the intended reading.
- A future obligation that needs the filesystem stays out of the kit: it is a
  verb or a bundle script (`tools/uncovered.ts:1-13`).
